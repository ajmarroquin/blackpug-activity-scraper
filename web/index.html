Black Pug Registrant Export

Export your unit's event registrations from Black Pug to an Excel file: one tab per event, with booked and unfinished registrations listed separately. It runs in your browser, so there's nothing to install.

  1. Add the button to your bookmarks bar

    Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. If you don't see the bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on a Mac).

    Export from Black Pug Drag it to your bookmarks bar. Clicking it here won't do anything.

  2. Log in to Black Pug and pick your unit

    Open your council's event page (for example scoutingevent.com/640-…) and log in. Click your name in the top right and pick your Pack or Troop.

  3. Click the bookmark

    A new tab opens with a summary of your events. Pick a date range and download the Excel file.

Your data stays on your computer

The bookmark reads the registration list already on your screen and hands it to this page inside your browser. Your Black Pug password and your registrants' names and emails never go to a server. This page has no server code, and its security settings block it from connecting to other sites.

Troubleshooting
Nothing happens when I click the bookmark
Your browser may have blocked the new tab. Allow pop-ups for scoutingevent.com, then click the bookmark again.
"No registrations found"
Make sure you're logged in and have picked your unit from the user menu. You can also open View Activity yourself before clicking the bookmark.
Can I use it on my phone?
Not easily. Phone browsers make bookmarks like this hard to use. Use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer.
Something looks wrong in the spreadsheet
Black Pug may have changed their page. Open an issue and say what you expected to see. Please don't include anyone's personal details.
